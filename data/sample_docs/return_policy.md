# Returns and refunds

Customers may return an unused item within 30 days of delivery. The item must be in its original packaging and include proof of purchase.

## Damaged items

If an item arrives damaged, contact Support within 7 days of delivery with the order number and a photo of the damage. Support will review the claim and explain the available replacement or refund options. A refund is not automatic until the claim is approved.

## Refund timing

After an approved return is received, refunds are issued to the original payment method. Allow 5 to 10 business days for the payment provider to post the refund.
